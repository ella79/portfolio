Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 September 2026

Thank you for visiting my portfolio. This policy explains what information this site handles, why, and how it is protected.

01Information collection

This site has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Personal information is collected only in one case:

  • Contact form. The site itself stores nothing. If you choose to send a message, your name, email address, subject and message are delivered to me through Formspree, so I can reply. Formspree also keeps a copy in the submission history of my account, and I delete messages once they are no longer needed.

Like any website, some technical information is also processed automatically when a page loads:

  • IP address
  • browser type and version
  • date and time of the request

02Use of information

The information is used only to:

  • read and answer the message you sent
  • deliver the pages and keep the site secure

I do not sell it, share it for marketing or use it to build profiles of visitors.

03Cookies

This site does not use cookies. If you switch between the light and dark theme, your choice is saved in your own browser's local storage so the site remembers it. It never leaves your device, and you can remove it by clearing the site data in your browser.

04Third-party services

This site relies on a few external services, which may process data under their own policies:

  • Formspree receives contact form messages and forwards them to my inbox. Formspree privacy policy
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and processes technical request data. GitHub privacy statement
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces and receives your IP address when the fonts load. Google privacy policy
  • LinkedIn and GitHub links take you to those sites, where their own policies apply.

Some of these providers process data outside the European Union, under the safeguards described in their policies.

05Data protection

Messages are kept only as long as our conversation needs them, and deleted when you ask. The site is served over HTTPS, and the form includes protection against automated spam.

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete the data I hold about you, or object to its use. I will answer within 30 days. You can also complain to the Romanian data protection authority, ANSPDCP.

06Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always on this page, with its effective date.

07Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, feel free to reach out to me via a message.
