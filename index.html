A little about me

More than a decade of quality assurance engineering

I'm Emanuela Telescu, a Senior QA Automation Engineer based in Timisoara, Romania. With more than 15 years in quality assurance, 6 in test automation and 2 in AI augmented testing, I have spent my career making sure the software teams deliver is software people can rely on.

To me, quality is a standard worth living by, at work and in everyday life.

I like being early to what is changing in test automation. I moved a production suite from Cypress to Playwright and built the new framework myself, and today I explore QA at the edge of AI, building my own agents and skills and pushing them to see what testing can become.

My strength is turning testing into something a team can depend on: stable suites instead of flaky ones, checks that run inside the pipeline, and automation that stays easy to maintain as the product grows.

Beyond the technical side, I am passionate about sharing what I know, learning every day, and trying out new technologies and ideas. Most of all, I like building things that are genuinely useful, making AI an ally that takes over the repetitive tasks of the day, so we have more time left for ourselves, as people.

Agentic Playwright Suite

End to end, visual and REST API tests against Automation Exercise, in Playwright and TypeScript. Claude Code agents write and repair the tests over Playwright MCP. The pipeline runs them with no AI in the loop, inside an image it builds first, and publishes the reports on every push to main.
